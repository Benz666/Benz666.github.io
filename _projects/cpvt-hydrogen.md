---
title: Spectral splitting for concentrated photovoltaic/thermal–hydrogen systems
short: CPV/T–hydrogen with spectral splitting
group: earlier
order: 6
period: 2020 – 2024
status: Published
question: >-
  Photovoltaic cells use only part of the solar spectrum and lose efficiency when hot. Can a
  photocatalyst nanofluid filter send UV and near-infrared light to heat and hydrogen production,
  while passing the useful band to the solar cell?
methods:
  - Pt@TiO₂ photocatalyst suspensions as liquid optical filters
  - Design of solar collector tubes and liquid spherical lenses as secondary concentrators
  - Optical, thermal and hydrogen-production experiments; COMSOL simulation
contributions:
  - Designed and built the CPVT-H system (first author, Renewable Energy 2022)
  - Contributed to the secondary-concentrator, desalination and demonstration-system work, including the 50-unit demonstration project in Tacheng, Xinjiang
findings: Published in Renewable Energy (2022), Applied Energy (2023) and Energy Conversion and Management (2024).
