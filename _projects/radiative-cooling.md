title: Passive radiative cooling materials
short: Passive radiative cooling
group: earlier
order: 5
period: 2022 – 2024
status: Published
question: >-
  Can low-cost polymer–dielectric films reflect sunlight and emit strongly in the infrared well enough
  to cool below ambient temperature in summer daytime, and help keep solar cells cool?
methods:
  - Micro-nanoporous TPU/TiO₂ granular composite films
  - Optical characterization (spectrophotometry, FT-IR) and outdoor cooling tests
  - Application to photovoltaic thermal management
contributions:
  - Designed and prepared the films and built the outdoor test setup (first author, ACS Applied Energy Materials 2024)
findings: Over 15 °C of passive radiative cooling below ambient, as reported in the ACS Applied Energy Materials paper.
