---
title: Phonon spectra and interfacial thermal transport at GaN/Al₂O₃ dislocation cores
short: GaN/Al₂O₃ dislocation phonons
group: phd
order: 1
period: Ongoing
status: Manuscript in preparation
question: >-
  Misfit dislocations at GaN/sapphire interfaces create local atomic structures (dislocation cores and
  bridge sites) whose vibrations differ from the bulk. How do these local phonon spectra change, how do
  they relate to electron energy-loss (EELS) vibrational spectroscopy, and what do they mean for heat
  crossing the interface?
methods:
  - Molecular dynamics with a foundation machine-learned interatomic potential (MACE)
  - Region-resolved phonon density of states and EELS-like spectra from velocity autocorrelation
  - Participation-ratio analysis of localized modes
  - Atomistic Green's function (AGF) calculation of elastic interfacial thermal conductance
contributions:
  - Built atomistic interface models and ran multi-seed MD on research clusters
  - Developed the spectral analysis and AGF transport workflow
findings: Results will be shared after publication.
---
