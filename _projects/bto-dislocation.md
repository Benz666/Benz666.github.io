---
title: Phonon direction asymmetry at BaTiO₃ dislocations
short: BaTiO₃ dislocation phonon asymmetry
group: phd
order: 5.5
period: Apr. 2025 – Present
status: Ongoing
question: >-
  Momentum-resolved vibrational EELS can see phonons travelling in opposite directions on the two sides
  of a dislocation in BaTiO₃. What sets the shape of this asymmetry, and can the spectrally resolved
  transmissivity of the defect be recovered from measured line profiles?
methods:
  - Deterministic phonon Boltzmann transport (JAX-BTE) with a partially transmitting, specular interface inside a single crystal
  - First-principles (TDEP) phonon properties resolved into the energy windows used by the EELS measurement
  - Point heat sources scanned across the interface to compute direction-resolved phonon populations in each window
  - Decay-model fitting and a calibrated inversion from asymmetry amplitude to interface transmissivity
contributions:
  - Built the simulation and analysis workflow, from material tables and meshes to window-resolved line profiles
  - Linked the decay length of each energy window to the phonon mean free path, and the amplitude to interface reflection
  - Designed and tested the transmissivity inversion on synthetic cases, and advised on fitting the experimental profiles
findings: >-
  In the simulations the asymmetry comes entirely from reflection at the interface: its decay length follows
  the phonon mean free path of each energy window, while its amplitude grows with the reflectivity, so the
  spectral transmissivity can be recovered from the amplitudes. Quantitative results will be shared after publication.
---
