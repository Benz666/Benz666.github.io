---
title: Thermal transport across SrTiO₃ tilt grain boundaries
short: SrTiO₃ tilt grain boundaries
group: phd
order: 5
period: Aug. 2026 – Present
status: Ongoing
question: >-
  Grain boundaries scatter phonons in oxide ceramics and thin films, but how strongly depends on their
  atomic structure. How does the tilt misorientation angle of a SrTiO₃ grain boundary control heat flow
  across it, and how do the local vibrational spectra at boundary sites differ from the bulk?
methods:
  - Non-equilibrium molecular dynamics (LAMMPS) across symmetric tilt grain boundaries of increasing misorientation angle
  - Zero-shot foundation machine-learning interatomic potential (MACE-MH-1), without grain-boundary fine-tuning
  - Region-resolved vibrational density of states (bulk, bridge and tilt-core sites) for comparison with vibrational EELS
contributions:
  - Built the grain-boundary transport cells and the NEMD workflow
  - Analyzed effective thermal conductivity and site-resolved vibrational spectra
findings: >-
  In the current simulations the effective thermal conductivity decreases steadily as the tilt angle grows.
  Quantitative results will be shared after publication.
